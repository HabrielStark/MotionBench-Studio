# Research map and next validation steps

Study: HeyGen Code2Video benchmark for code-driven motion design, its dimensions and human preference protocol; VBench (video generation quality), VBench-2.0, EvalCrafter, VideoScore, and VQAScore for model-based video evaluation. They are not interchangeable with production motion-design QA. Also inspect community Claude/Remotion pipelines, `howseen-ai/claude-motion-design`, `Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft`, `JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase`, `jordanrendric/claude-video-vision`, and Remotion official skills. Verify licenses and reproducibility before incorporating code.

Community posts on X/Reddit are anecdotal. Claims about Claude Opus 5.5 quality require date-stamped reproducible examples; a universal improvement or guaranteed quality score is unsupported. Benchmark validity needs a human-labeled held-out dataset and ablation studies with/without reference and critique loops.
